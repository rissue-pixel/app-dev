# My Favorite Anime Series: One Piece

Netflix: [Netflix - One Piece](https://www.netflix.com/title/81002111)

*One Piece* is a legendary anime/series about **Monkey D. Luffy**, a pirate with the power to stretch like rubber, and he's on a quest to find the legendary treasure **One Piece**, and become *King of the Pirates*.

## Straw Hat Crew: (**SPOILER ALERT!**)
- **Luffy** – The fearless *Captain* who ate the *Gomu-Gomu no Mi* a.k.a **Human-Human Fruit, Mythical Zoan Type: Nika** who's on a quest to find the **One Piece** and be the *Freest Man Alive*.
- **Zoro** – The *Vice-Captain* of the Crew, and a wielder and user of *3-Swordsman Style*. Who's making his name to be the **Greatest Swordsman** ever.
- **Nami** – The genius *Navigator* who initially joins the crew for selfish reasons but later becomes one of Luffy’s most trusted allies. She’s obsessed with money, tangerine, and treasure, but she’s also an expert cartographer who’s helping Luffy reach the Grand Line.
- **Usopp** – The *Sharpshooter* and self-proclaimed "Brave warrior of the sea" who often lies but proves to be incredibly courageous. He starts as a coward but grows to become a true member of the crew, especially after the emotional events at **Sniper Island**.
- **Sanji** – The charming *Cook* and martial artist who uses powerful kicks in battle. He’s also on a journey to find the **All Blue**, a legendary sea where all the world’s oceans meet. Sanji has a tragic backstory involving his family and the **Vinsmoke** family, who he despises.
- **Chopper** – A reindeer who ate the *Hito Hito no Mi* aka Human-Human Fruit and can transform into different forms. He’s the crew’s *Doctor* and has a huge heart, though he used to be lonely and rejected before meeting Luffy.
- **Robin** – The calm and intelligent *Archaeologist* who seeks the truth behind the lost history of the world, specifically the **Poneglyphs**. She’s a former assassin and was once a member of the criminal group **Baroque Works**, but her dream is to uncover the **True History**, which links to the ancient **Void Century**.
- **Franky** – The cyborg *Shipwright* who built their awesome ship, the **Thousand Sunny**.
- **Brook** – The living skeleton *Musician* who ate the *Yomi Yomi no Mi*, allowing him to come back to life after dying. He’s been alive for decades, traveling alone for a long time, and is former crew of the **Rumbar Pirates**.
- **Jinbe** – The fish-man *Helmsman* who joined the crew after the events of **Fish-Man Island**. Formerly a **Warlord of the Sea**, he has a deep sense of honor and is a master of Fish-Man Karate. Jinbe helps Luffy and the crew in major battles, especially during the **Wano Arc** and the **Whole Cake Island Arc**.
